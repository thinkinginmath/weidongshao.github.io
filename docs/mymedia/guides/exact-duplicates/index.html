Practical guide · Exact duplicate files

Review identical photo copies on your Mac.

MyMedia compares exact file contents across your indexed folders. You choose which copy stays and review the others before moving them to Trash.

Exact copies and similar shots are different

A photo copied into two folders can be byte-for-byte identical. A resized JPEG, edited export, burst shot or HEIC-to-JPEG conversion usually has different bytes even when it looks similar. Current exact review does not group those variants. Similar-shot review remains in development.

Review a group

  1. Add your photo folders and let fingerprinting finish. Open Exact duplicates.
  2. Inspect each group’s filenames and source folders. One copy is marked to keep. Choose Keep this one instead if you prefer another location.
  3. Select the extra copies for review. The bar shows your selected count, Archive, Move to Trash and Clear selection.
  4. Check the selected copies and keeper again before choosing Move to Trash. Do not remove a copy you still need in a particular folder or backup.

What the space estimate means

The estimate totals extra file sizes. It is not storage already recovered. Files in Trash may continue occupying space, and shared filesystem storage can affect the amount eventually recovered. MyMedia does not empty Trash.

Safety and scope

Organization’s journaled undo is separate from Trash operations. Keep an independent backup. Library verification warnings can pause cleanup; review the issue instead of accepting unknown information just to proceed.
