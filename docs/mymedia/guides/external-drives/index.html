Practical guide · Mac folders and drives

Your photo folders, together in one view.

Keep the folders you have. Add connected sources to browse their photos together, then decide whether to organize in place or gather verified copies.

1. Connect and add your folders

Connect the old drive and confirm you can open its folder in Finder. In MyMedia, use Folders → Add and choose a regular folder. Add other Mac folders the same way. Photo libraries and application bundles are separate from ordinary folder indexing.

2. Start with a concrete question

Browse All photos or select one source. Search a filename, a year, or recognized text after indexing finishes. Capture-date and filename search are useful starting points. Experimental visual ranking can miss relevant photos; it is not proof that a photo is absent.

3. Review exact copies

Open Exact duplicates, compare folders and choose which copy to keep. Keepers for removal must have connected originals; a saved preview from a disconnected drive is not a verified backup.

Detailed keeper and removal steps →

4. Preview organization

Open Organize folders, choose a destination, then choose text/filter matches, selected photos, or the whole library. Preview the proposed dated folders and space needed. Photos without a reliable capture date go to Date unknown - Review. Review before choosing Copy or Move. Keep an independent backup.

Optional: bring scattered folders into one archive

If you prefer a new consolidated archive, choose Back up → Bring your photos together. Add local source folders, choose an archive and copy missing files with content verification. Preserve a verified second copy on another drive before reviewing duplicates or applying dated organization. Rerunning resumes the project; coverage is checked again after changes.

Gathering copies files; it is not indexing in place. The guide does not delete sources or certify that a drive is safe to erase. Phone, Drive and chat media first need explicitly chosen local copies.

If a drive is missing or access is denied

A disconnected folder can retain a saved listing and cached previews. They may be stale, and previews are not full originals or backups. Reconnect the drive before opening, copying, moving, editing or trashing originals. An “Access needed” label means check MyMedia’s access in macOS Privacy & Security, then rescan in Settings. A drive mounting under a new name may need to be added at its new path.

Requirements and limits

Build 80 targets Apple Silicon and macOS 14+. Mounted local folders are the ordinary library. Apple Photos and Google Drive remain separate browsers. Physical unplug/replug, exFAT and very large libraries still need broader outside acceptance testing.
