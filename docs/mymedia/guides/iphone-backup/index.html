Practical guide · iPhone storage

Back up imported iPhone photos before clearing space.

Import selected photos and videos into a folder on your Mac, make a verified backup, and check it before deciding what to remove from your phone.

Coming in the next beta build (not in build 80): Back up ▾ → iPhone: organize and back up copies photos, videos and Live Photo companions directly over USB into a new dated folder, without Image Capture, then offers an organize step with undo and the same verified copy. It is copy only and never deletes anything on the phone. Its coverage check compares names and sizes; that is not proof the files are identical, and it is never advice to delete from the phone. These steps describe build 80, which uses Image Capture.

1. Import into a folder you can find

Connect and unlock your iPhone, and approve its trust prompt yourself if requested. In Apple Image Capture, choose a dedicated destination such as Pictures / iPhone import, select the files and click Download. Keep deletion during import off.

MyMedia guides this workflow but does not control Image Capture or guarantee its import completed. If Image Capture stalls or reports errors, keep the phone originals and resolve the import before relying on the backup.

2. Keep Live Photo resources together

A Live Photo needs its still image (HEIC or JPEG) and MOV companion. Keep any imported AAE sidecars too. A still image alone is not a complete Live Photo. MyMedia’s folder backup preserves regular files that are actually present; it cannot prove a missing companion was imported or guarantee re-import into Apple Photos.

3. Choose a second copy

Local: select the imported folder and a destination on another connected drive. MyMedia checks copied files with SHA-256, writes a receipt and does not replace existing destination files. These local copies are not encrypted by MyMedia. Repeating the copy resumes it.

Optional cloud: sign into encrypted backup with Google, retain your recovery phrase, unlock the backup and choose the imported folder. Contents, filenames and metadata are encrypted on the Mac before incremental upload. This service is separate from Google Drive. It is experimental: keep another copy and test restore.

4. Verify before deleting from the phone

Read the result and any skipped-file warnings. Open sample images and videos from the backup, check Live Photo companions, and test cloud restore if used. File verification covers the chosen folder, not the entire phone library. MyMedia does not delete iPhone photos. If iCloud Photos is enabled, understand its synchronized deletion behavior before removing anything.

Troubleshooting the import and backup

  • iPhone is missing: unlock it, confirm Trust on the phone, use a data-capable cable and check Image Capture before opening MyMedia’s guide.
  • Some originals remain in iCloud: Image Capture may not have every original. Resolve that in Apple’s tools and verify the import; MyMedia cannot prove complete phone coverage.
  • Destination does not fit: the copy review shows source bytes, free destination space and space needed for new files. Choose another drive or a smaller source; existing destination files are never replaced.
  • Skipped links or placeholders: review the receipt. MyMedia does not follow symlinks or silently download online-only source files.
  • Copy interrupted: reconnect the same source and destination and repeat. Already matching files are checked and retained; conflicts are reported.
  • Before removing phone items: open photo and video samples from the second drive and compare counts/resources. If iCloud Photos is enabled, deleting on one device can propagate through Apple’s sync.
