MyMedia Photos: what the current beta does.

MyMedia makes the photo folders you already have searchable and manageable, without creating another library or taking control of your originals.

Latest tested buildVersion 0.1.0, build 80 · October 16, 2026
PlatformmacOS 14 or later. The current tested build is for Apple Silicon (M-series Macs). Older Intel builds have limitations; they are not build 80.
DistributionDeveloper ID signed and notarized by Apple. Developer certificate: Weidong Shao. Public Apple Silicon beta, distributed outside the Mac App Store.
Local libraryExisting folders and connected drives, indexed in place. No separate managed library is required and no account required for local folder features.
SearchFilenames, capture dates, recognized text, content labels and user-assigned people. Local visual search is experimental; it does not guarantee every relevant photo will be found. Face recognition needs a local model and manual analysis.
DuplicatesByte-for-byte exact duplicate review with keeper selection and a space estimate. Similar-shot grouping is outside exact-copy review.
File changesOrganization previews copy/move destinations and journals operations for undo. Editing saves renditions. Cleanup moves chosen files to Trash; it does not permanently delete them or empty Trash.
iPhone workflowImport through Apple Image Capture into a Mac folder, then create and verify another copy. MyMedia does not import directly from the phone or delete phone assets.
Optional sourcesApple Photos and Google Drive are separate browsers, not automatic folder-library indexing. Drive access is read-only and account-wide; chosen originals can be copied to the Mac.
Optional cloud backupExperimental. Google account sign-in, with file contents, names and metadata encrypted before upload. Separate from Google Drive. Keep the recovery phrase and another copy.
PriceThe first public local-app release is planned to be free. Cloud storage allowances and pricing have not been announced.

Formats and requirements

The folder photo library recognizes JPEG/JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, AVIF, HEIC/HEIF and TIFF/TIF. Decoding, native-model support and metadata vary by format; animated images are not a video workspace. macOS 14+ and Apple Silicon are required for this public package. No Intel build is advertised here.

How search behaves

Filename, capture-date, people labels, recognized text and content labels are combined locally. OCR/content search depends on indexing. Experimental visual ranking may add guessed matches and may miss relevant photos. Organize’s view scope includes deterministic text/filter matches; select visual matches explicitly to organize them. No measured relevance or similar-shot guarantee is claimed.

Pricing

The local beta is available without payment; the first public local-app release is planned to be free. Cloud-storage allowances and pricing are not announced. Third-party services may charge separately.

Privacy boundaries

Local photo analysis does not upload photos. An automatic daily update check contacts uuBright’s service unless disabled in About. Google Drive, place lookup, S3 copies, encrypted backup and portrait generation each have separate data boundaries. The website loads no analytics tags. Optional release-update email signup is separate from app use.

Common questions

Does MyMedia require importing into Apple Photos?

No. Its folder library indexes existing local folders and connected drives in place. Apple Photos is an optional, separate read-only browser.

Does exact duplicate review find similar photos?

No. Exact review compares file contents byte for byte. Resized exports, edited versions, burst shots and visually similar images may have different contents and are outside this feature. This review does not choose a best shot.

Does backup verification prove my entire iPhone is backed up?

No. MyMedia verifies the files in the imported folder. It cannot confirm Image Capture imported every phone asset or every Live Photo resource. Keep the phone originals until you independently check your backup.

Is MyMedia an App Store app?

The current beta is distributed separately from the Mac App Store. Build 80 for Apple Silicon is Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple; notarization is not an endorsement or an App Store review.
