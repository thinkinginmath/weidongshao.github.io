MyMedia Photos: what the current beta does.

Understand and organize the photo archive you already own, without moving it into another library. Your originals stay ordinary files in your folders; MyMedia keeps its own index and settings beside them on your Mac.

This page describes build 80, the build you can download today. Features finished since then are listed separately under Coming in the next beta build; they are not in build 80.

Current download and beta boundaries

As of November 4, 2026, the public download remains version 0.1.0, build 80. The homepage’s Space report screenshot is a preview of the next-beta renderer using sample figures, not a scan of a real customer library or space already freed.

What the sample report counts

The 60 GB sample library contains 36 GB of photos and 24 GB of large videos. Of the photos, 18 GB are exact copies, 2 GB are older screenshots and 16 GB are other photos. Its 20 GB review total includes only the exact copies and older screenshots. Large videos are shown separately and do not increase that total. Similar shots need a keeper choice first; copied-to-drive files are report-only in this flow.

Storage and cleanup

The app scans in place and can copy straight to an external drive, but it still needs room for its index and previews. Low-disk checks currently protect selected organize, undo, Backup copy and restore paths; broader write coverage, cache relocation and video duplicate cleanup remain in development. “Works at any free-space level” is not a promise of this beta. Moving files to Trash does not immediately release their disk space; macOS releases it when you choose to empty the Trash. Keep an independent backup before cleanup.

Coming in the next beta

  • Space report after the first scan: counted totals follow the exact photo copies and older screenshots the review can offer. The public build 80 download does not include this report.
  • Copy, verify, then Trash: cross-drive organize moves verify the destination and recheck the source before system Trash. Interrupted recovery and drive-identity edge cases remain under validation; this is not a claim about every move path in build 80.
  • “Nothing lost” receipts: organize, reviewed cleanup and Move to Trash get History records and CSV export. The status requires a finished step, a trusted journal, a recorded outcome for every file and none needing attention. Manual Trash does not imply another identical copy exists.
  • Photo Drive setup and direct iPhone import: guided setup and copy-only USB import of photos, videos and companions are being tested for a downloadable Mac release. In build 80, use Apple Image Capture to import first.

Organize and backup workflow

The next beta adds Organize everything: home, scan, free space, add sources, review copies, organize and back up. Its folder-backup wizard starts with an ordinary source folder, without an iPhone import step. A folder survey groups subdirectories under their parent and offers enlarged sample previews; the library scan limit is 12 levels below the selected folder.

A November 7 local Mac test copied five HEIC photos (2.8 MB) into dated folders, created a verified same-disk backup and rechecked all five against its receipt. Independent hashes matched both copies and the unchanged originals. This small internal-disk test does not establish separate-drive restore, interrupted transfer, all formats, complete phone coverage or large Takeout acceptance. The tested development app was ad-hoc signed; it is not the signed build-80 download.

Sources and supporting tools

The full source list on the homepage is a launch plan, not a claim that every source is available in build 80. Google Takeout import is coming soon and reads export files downloaded from Google; it is separate from the optional read-only Google Drive connection. Apple Photos stays browse-only in the current download. Messages needs user-enabled Full Disk Access; Messages and WeChat checks remain experimental and never write to their source storage. Phone and chat imports never authorize source deletion.

Visual search and faces remain experimental. Index and thumbnail usage can be measured in development, but a universal 3% footprint limit and relocating the cache to the Photo Drive are not implemented. Final pricing and any early-user offer will be announced when licensing is ready.

These development features still need packaged Mac and real-library acceptance. The build 80 download and checksum are unchanged.

Formats and requirements

The folder photo library recognizes JPEG/JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, AVIF, HEIC/HEIF and TIFF/TIF. Decoding, native-model support and metadata vary by format; animated images are not a video workspace. macOS 14+ and Apple Silicon are required for this public package. No Intel build is advertised here.

How search behaves

Filename, capture-date, people labels, recognized text and content labels are combined locally. OCR/content search depends on indexing. Experimental visual ranking may add guessed matches and may miss relevant photos. Organize’s view scope includes deterministic text/filter matches; select visual matches explicitly to organize them. No measured relevance or similar-shot guarantee is claimed.

Privacy boundaries

Local photo analysis does not upload photos. An automatic daily update check contacts uuBright’s service unless disabled in About. Place-name lookup, Google Drive, S3 folder copies (not a backup: no MyMedia encryption or restore), encrypted cloud backup and portrait generation are opt-in and each have separate data boundaries. The website loads no analytics tags. Optional release-update email signup is separate from app use.

Common questions

Does MyMedia require importing into Apple Photos?

No. Its folder library indexes existing local folders and connected drives in place. Apple Photos is an optional, separate read-only browser.

Does exact duplicate review find similar photos?

No. Exact review compares file contents byte for byte. Resized exports, edited versions, burst shots and visually similar images may have different contents and are outside this feature. This review does not choose a best shot.

Does backup verification prove my entire iPhone is backed up?

No. MyMedia verifies the files in the imported folder. It cannot confirm Image Capture imported every phone asset or every Live Photo resource. Keep the phone originals until you independently check your backup.

Will MyMedia tell me it is safe to delete photos from my phone?

No. MyMedia never deletes anything on the phone and never gives deletion advice. In the next beta build, the iPhone coverage check compares names and sizes; matching names and sizes is a useful sign, not proof the files are identical.

Are albums or collections a backup?

No. A collection is a list of references to photos where they already are. Saving one never copies a file, and deleting one never removes a file.

Is MyMedia an App Store app?

The current beta is distributed separately from the Mac App Store. Build 80 for Apple Silicon is Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple; notarization is not an endorsement or an App Store review.
