MyMedia Photos: what the current beta does.

Understand and organize the photo archive you already own, without moving it into another library. Your originals stay ordinary files in your folders; MyMedia keeps its own index and settings beside them on your Mac.

This page describes build 80, the build you can download today. Features finished since then are listed separately under Coming in the next beta build; they are not in build 80.

PlatformmacOS 14 or later. The current tested build is for Apple Silicon (M-series Macs). Older Intel builds have limitations; they are not build 80.
DistributionDeveloper ID signed and notarized by Apple. Developer certificate: Weidong Shao. Public Apple Silicon beta, distributed outside the Mac App Store.
Local libraryExisting folders and connected drives, indexed in place. No separate managed library is required and no account required for local folder features.
SearchFilenames, capture dates, recognized text, content labels and user-assigned people. Local visual search is experimental; it does not guarantee every relevant photo will be found. Face recognition needs a local model and manual analysis.
DuplicatesByte-for-byte exact duplicate review with keeper selection and a space estimate. Similar shots are not part of exact-copy review; similar-shot review is coming in the next beta build.
File changesOrganization previews copy/move destinations and journals operations for undo. Editing saves renditions. Cleanup moves chosen files to Trash; it does not permanently delete them or empty Trash.
Backup copyCopy a folder to another drive. Every copied file is checked with SHA-256 and a receipt is saved beside the copy. Nothing at the destination is replaced; running it again resumes. Links and online-only placeholders are reported, not followed or downloaded. Local copies are not encrypted by MyMedia.
iPhone workflowIn build 80: import through Apple Image Capture into a Mac folder, then create and verify another copy. Build 80 does not import directly from the phone. MyMedia never deletes phone assets. Direct copy-only USB import is coming in the next beta build.
Optional sourcesExperimental. Apple Photos, Google Drive, Apple Messages and WeChat are separate browsers, not automatic folder-library indexing. Apple Photos browsing is read-only. Drive access is read-only and account-wide; chosen originals can be copied to the Mac. Messages needs Full Disk Access; Messages and WeChat storage are never written to.
Optional cloud backupExperimental. Backs up a folder you choose to MyMedia’s encrypted storage (backup.uubright.com). You sign in with Google, but the backup is not stored in Google Drive. File contents, filenames and metadata are encrypted on your Mac before upload; the service sees your account identity, opaque keys, ciphertext sizes, counts and timing. Your recovery phrase never reaches the service. The format has not had an independent security review; keep the recovery phrase and another copy.
Experimental in this betaEncrypted cloud backup, Google Drive, WeChat, Apple Messages, Apple Photos browsing, visual search and face recognition. They work, but have had less testing; expect rough edges.
PriceThe first public local-app release is planned to be free. Cloud storage allowances and pricing have not been announced.

Coming in the next beta build

Not in build 80, and not downloadable yet. These features are built and in testing. We will announce the build that includes them here and on the download page.

  • Organize and back up your iPhone. One guided flow in three steps: Import, Organize, Back up. Copy photos, videos and Live Photo companions directly over USB into a new dated folder (Apple Image Capture remains an option); organize a complete copy into year and month folders, with undo; then make a verified drive copy and/or an encrypted cloud backup. Copy only: nothing on the phone is deleted. An optional coverage check compares the phone’s items with the folder by name and size. That is a heuristic, not proof the files are identical, and MyMedia never advises deleting from the phone. Direct import still needs testing on real phones.
  • Similar shots. Repeated takes from the same camera within seconds are grouped. MyMedia suggests one to keep, mainly by sharpness and exposure, and says why; Compare shows the takes side by side with one shared zoom. You choose; nothing is removed automatically.
  • Check or restore a backup. Re-check a Backup copy against its receipt without the original drive, with missing, changed and unreadable files listed. Restore copies files out only after each matches the receipt, and never replaces anything at the destination.
  • Free up space becomes a Space view: totals per library folder, and an explicit read-only check of Downloads, Desktop, Messages and WeChat for copies of photos the library already keeps. Removal stays in the reviewed cleanup.
  • Use this drive for your photos. Adding a drive root offers a setup: drive facts, a survey of what is on the drive, then Photo Library and Photo Backups folders at its top level. Very large folders open a read-only survey before indexing. A drive card in Settings shows a drive’s folders, its backup copies and how many photos exist only on that drive.
  • Suggested albums. Trips and events are offered as albums with their photos ticked and a name filled in. An album is a list of references, not a copy or a backup.
  • Videos in library folders are listed and playable in Photos. Dark mode and a new app icon. The first run needs no model download; face recognition and dish recognition offer their downloads when you choose them.

Formats and requirements

The folder photo library recognizes JPEG/JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP, AVIF, HEIC/HEIF and TIFF/TIF. Decoding, native-model support and metadata vary by format; animated images are not a video workspace. macOS 14+ and Apple Silicon are required for this public package. No Intel build is advertised here.

How search behaves

Filename, capture-date, people labels, recognized text and content labels are combined locally. OCR/content search depends on indexing. Experimental visual ranking may add guessed matches and may miss relevant photos. Organize’s view scope includes deterministic text/filter matches; select visual matches explicitly to organize them. No measured relevance or similar-shot guarantee is claimed.

Pricing

The local beta is available without payment; the first public local-app release is planned to be free. Cloud-storage allowances and pricing are not announced. Third-party services may charge separately.

Privacy boundaries

Local photo analysis does not upload photos. An automatic daily update check contacts uuBright’s service unless disabled in About. Place-name lookup, Google Drive, S3 folder copies (not a backup: no MyMedia encryption or restore), encrypted cloud backup and portrait generation are opt-in and each have separate data boundaries. The website loads no analytics tags. Optional release-update email signup is separate from app use.

Common questions

Does MyMedia require importing into Apple Photos?

No. Its folder library indexes existing local folders and connected drives in place. Apple Photos is an optional, separate read-only browser.

Does exact duplicate review find similar photos?

No. Exact review compares file contents byte for byte. Resized exports, edited versions, burst shots and visually similar images may have different contents and are outside this feature. This review does not choose a best shot.

Does backup verification prove my entire iPhone is backed up?

No. MyMedia verifies the files in the imported folder. It cannot confirm Image Capture imported every phone asset or every Live Photo resource. Keep the phone originals until you independently check your backup.

Will MyMedia tell me it is safe to delete photos from my phone?

No. MyMedia never deletes anything on the phone and never gives deletion advice. In the next beta build, the iPhone coverage check compares names and sizes; matching names and sizes is a useful sign, not proof the files are identical.

Are albums or collections a backup?

No. A collection is a list of references to photos where they already are. Saving one never copies a file, and deleting one never removes a file.

Is MyMedia an App Store app?

The current beta is distributed separately from the Mac App Store. Build 80 for Apple Silicon is Developer ID signed and notarized by Apple; notarization is not an endorsement or an App Store review.
