What you can do in early testing

From scattered photos to a clearer collection.

FIND

Find the photo, not the filename.

Browse people, capture dates and content labels. Search words recognized inside receipts, signs and documents. Face recognition needs an optional local model; visual search is experimental.

Example: find a receipt by its printed text.

CLEAN

Choose which copies to keep.

Compare exact duplicates and review the actual files before moving selected copies to Trash. Similar-shot grouping is coming next.

Compare → choose a keeper → review.

ORGANIZE

Give your folders some order.

Preview destinations before copying or moving photos into folders. Journaled organization supports undo. Nothing moves until you approve the plan.

Preview a date-based folder structure.

IMPROVE

Make a better copy.

Enhance, rotate or remove a background while keeping the original. Object erase is available with an additional local model.

Original → edit → separate rendition.

Let AI propose. You decide.

Connect a compatible AI assistant to search library metadata and propose cleanup plans. You review and apply changes inside MyMedia. Local analysis stays on your Mac; an external assistant follows the connection and privacy choices you make.

Before you get started

Simple answers.

Is MyMedia free?

The first public release will be free. There is no MyMedia subscription planned for the first release. Optional third-party services may charge separately.

Do I need an account?

No account is needed for local photos. Connecting Google Drive is optional and requires your Google sign-in.

Can I download it now?

MyMedia Photos is being tested on macOS. A public download will follow release testing and Mac signing/notarization. Contact us about early testing.

Does it upload my photos?

Local indexing and analysis don’t upload photos. Optional place lookup, cloud portrait generation, and S3 backup use external services when you choose them. See the privacy policy for the data each feature sends.

Does Google Drive mean Google Photos?

No. This integration browses files in Google Drive. It doesn’t provide access to your Google Photos library or private YouTube videos.
