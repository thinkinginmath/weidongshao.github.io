Put photos into folders that make sense.

Choose a destination and a scope. Review capture dates, proposed paths and the destination’s available space. Then approve Copy or Move.

Originals never move silently. Operations are journaled for undo; changed copies are kept rather than blindly removed. Unknown capture dates are set aside for review.

A second copy you can check

Back up a folder, and know every file arrived.

Copy a folder to another drive. MyMedia checks every copied file against the original with SHA-256 and saves a receipt beside the copy. Nothing already at the destination is replaced, and running it again resumes.

For iPhone photos, import with Apple Image Capture into a folder, then make the same verified copy.

A verified copy covers the files in the chosen folder, not your whole phone. Local copies are not encrypted by MyMedia. MyMedia never deletes anything on the phone.

Organize and back up your iPhone

One guided flow: copy photos, videos and Live Photo companions directly over USB into a new dated folder, organize a complete copy with undo, then make a verified drive copy or an encrypted cloud backup.

Copy only: nothing on the phone is deleted. The coverage check compares names and sizes with the phone. It is a heuristic, not proof the files are identical, and never advice to delete from the phone.

Similar shots, side by side

Repeated takes of the same moment are grouped. MyMedia suggests one to keep and shows why; Compare puts the takes side by side with one shared zoom. You choose.

The suggestion is a starting point, not a verdict. Nothing is removed automatically; removal is the same reviewed Move to Trash.

Check or restore a backup

Re-check a backup copy against its receipt, even when the original drive is gone. Restore copies files back only after each one matches the receipt.

A check reads the backup; it never changes it. A restore never replaces files at its destination.

See where your space went

Free up space shows totals per library folder and can check Downloads, Desktop, Messages and WeChat for copies of photos your library already keeps.

Report only. Removal stays in the reviewed cleanup, and nothing is ever written to Messages or WeChat. Messages and WeChat checks are experimental.

One drive for your photos

Adding a whole drive offers a setup: a survey of what is on it, then a Photo Library folder and a Photo Backups folder. A drive card shows its folders, backup copies and how many photos exist only on that drive.

Counts and surveys inform you; they never authorize cleanup.

Albums from trips and events

Trips and events found from capture time and location are offered as albums. Untick photos, rename, save.

An album is a list of references to photos where they are. It is not a copy or a backup.

Other sources Experimental

Apple Photos, Google Drive, Apple Messages and WeChat open as separate browsers with their own permissions. From Drive, Messages and WeChat you can save copies of the photos you choose into an ordinary folder; Apple Photos is browse-only.

Google Drive and Apple Photos access are read-only. Nothing is written to Messages or WeChat storage.

Encrypted cloud backup Experimental

Back up a folder to MyMedia’s encrypted storage. Files are encrypted on your Mac first, with a recovery phrase only you keep. You sign in with Google; your backup is not stored in Google Drive.

The format has not had an independent security review yet. Keep a drive copy too, and test restore.
