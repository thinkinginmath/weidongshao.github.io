One library. Three steps.

A home for your photos.
Room for everything else.

  1. 01

    Scan what you have.

    Add Mac folders and connected drives. The scan reads your files where they are. Review identical copies before choosing what to keep.

    iPhone in today’s download: import with Apple Image Capture first. Direct USB import and expanded WeChat checks are in testing.

  2. 02

    Keep a verified drive copy.

    Copy a folder to your external drive. Backup copy checks every file with SHA-256 and saves a receipt. Existing destination files are never replaced.

    A copy alone does not free space. Keep a second independent copy before cleanup; review every removal.

  3. 03

    Browse, even unplugged.

    Browse remembered photos through thumbnails on your Mac when the drive is disconnected. Reconnect the drive to open the full-size originals.

    Originals remain ordinary files on your drive. Your Mac keeps the index and cached previews.

A checkable copy.

Backup copy compares each copied file with its original using SHA-256. A receipt records what completed and what was skipped. Re-run to resume.

You approve removal.

Exact-copy cleanup rechecks the extra copy and its keeper before Trash. Nothing is removed automatically, and MyMedia never empties your Trash.

Analysis on your Mac.

Local scanning, text recognition and face analysis run on this Mac. These tools do not upload your photos. Optional online tools have separate, explicit actions.

The Space report

A report after your first scan, with reviewable exact copies and older screenshots. Similar shots, large videos and already-copied files are shown separately when they cannot be counted toward cleanup.

Copy. Verify. Then Trash.

Cross-drive organize moves verify the destination and recheck the source before sending the original to the system Trash. If a check fails, the original stays.

Recovery after an interrupted move is still under validation.

“Nothing lost” receipts

Organize and cleanup steps get a History record with per-file outcomes and CSV export. “Nothing lost” appears only when the step finished, its journal verified and every file has an outcome with none needing attention.

Planned Takeout import will read the export files you download from Google, with support for their dates, places and albums. It is separate from the optional, read-only Google Drive connection.

Even the photos hiding in your Messages.

Messages attachments live in a protected folder. Browsing them needs Full Disk Access, which you choose whether to enable in macOS Settings when using Messages. MyMedia reads attachment files locally, not your conversations.
