What you can do in early testing

Less clutter. A backup you can check.

CLEAN UP

See which extra copies take up space.

Compare byte-for-byte identical files across your indexed folders. Choose the copy to keep and see an estimate of the extra copies’ size before reviewing what goes to Trash.

Compare copies → choose a keeper → review the space estimate.

BACK UP

Back up iPhone photos before clearing space.

Import selected photos and videos with Apple’s Image Capture, then copy them to another drive and verify the copies. MyMedia guides the steps; you decide what to remove from your phone afterward.

Import → copy → verify → check before deleting.

FIND

Find photos in the folders you already use.

Browse capture dates, content labels and trips. Search recognized text in receipts, signs and documents. Your folder library stays in place, with no proprietary library import.

Search recognized text or narrow by capture date.

ORGANIZE

Preview the move. Keep an undo.

See proposed folder destinations before copying or moving photos. Journaled organization supports undo. Use collections to group chosen photos without moving files.

Preview destinations → approve → undo if needed.

About the space estimate: it totals extra duplicate file sizes, not disk space already recovered. Moving files to Trash may not free space until Trash is emptied. Shared storage and filesystem behavior can also affect the result. MyMedia does not empty Trash for you.

Make room for new memories

Import from iPhone. Keep a backup.

A guided workflow takes you from Apple’s Image Capture to a backup destination you choose. Import selected photos and videos over USB into a folder on your Mac, then keep a second copy on a connected drive or use experimental encrypted cloud backup.

01 · IMPORT

Bring selected files onto your Mac.

Connect and unlock your iPhone, then download with Image Capture. Keep originals on the phone during import. Live Photos need both their image and video companion files.

02 · BACK UP

Choose where the copy lives.

Local backup copies every regular file in your selected folder, checks each copy’s SHA-256 hash and produces a receipt. Existing destination files are not overwritten. Local copies are not encrypted by MyMedia.

OPTIONAL · CLOUD

No external drive? Try encrypted backup.

Sign in with Google, save your recovery phrase and explicitly choose a folder. File contents, filenames and metadata are encrypted on your Mac before upload. Incremental uploads reuse unchanged data; restore saves verified files into a new folder.

Experimental beta · Keep another copy · Save your recovery phrase.

Check the backup before clearing your phone.

MyMedia does not delete iPhone photos or confirm that everything on your phone was imported. Review the result, verify the cloud backup if you use it, and open sample photos and videos before deciding what to remove yourself. Google sign-in cannot recover a lost recovery phrase.

Start where your photos live

Folders first. Other sources when you need them.

Index local folders and connected disks in place. Browse your System Photo Library through Apple’s read-only Photos interface after granting permission; this is a separate browser, not automatic folder indexing. Optional Google Drive browsing and chosen-file downloads are also in testing.

About Google Drive permissions

Google’s read-only grant covers all files in your Drive; choosing a folder does not narrow that grant. MyMedia does not change cloud files. Drive is separate from Google Photos, and signing in for encrypted backup does not grant Drive access. Read the data and permission details →

Before you get started

Simple answers.

Is MyMedia free?

The first public release of the local app is planned to be free. This does not promise free or unlimited cloud storage. Cloud backup allowances and pricing have not been announced; optional third-party services may charge separately.

Which Macs are supported?

macOS 14 or later. Apple Silicon is recommended. Intel builds do not support ONNX-backed visual search, face embeddings or object erase; broader Intel acceptance testing is still pending.

Do I need an account?

No account is needed for local browsing, organization or local backup. Experimental encrypted cloud backup requires Google sign-in. Google Drive is a separate optional connection.

Can I download it now?

Signed Mac test builds are available by request. The Apple Silicon test build is notarized; broader release acceptance checks remain pending before a public download. Contact us about early testing.

Does it upload my photos?

Local indexing and analysis don’t upload photos. Experimental encrypted cloud backup uploads encrypted files only after you choose a folder and start it. Automatic update checks contact uuBright’s servers unless disabled in About. Optional place lookup and S3 folder copies also use external services when selected. See the privacy policy for the data each feature sends.

Does Google Drive mean Google Photos?

No. This integration browses files in Google Drive. It doesn’t provide access to your Google Photos library.
