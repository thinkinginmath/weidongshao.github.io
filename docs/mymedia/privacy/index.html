MyMedia Photos · by uuBright

Privacy policy

Updated October 13, 2026

MyMedia Photos is a local-first Mac photo utility provided by uuBright. Your library is indexed and analyzed on your Mac. Connecting an external service is optional.

This policy covers MyMedia Photos, its optional Google Drive and experimental encrypted backup features, and this product website. The app is not yet available as a public download.

Local photos and analysis

When you add a folder, the app reads supported photos and creates a local index. Depending on the features you use, this includes file paths, file sizes and dates, content hashes, capture dates, camera details, GPS coordinates, recognized text, content labels, face thumbnails and embeddings, and names or labels you assign. Photo previews, editing renditions, settings, and operation history are also stored locally.

Local indexing, OCR, classification, face analysis, and editing run on your Mac. These functions do not send your library to uuBright. Face embeddings and associated labels remain local. The app indexes files in place; edits save separate copies. Organizing files or moving them to Trash requires your action.

Apple Photos and iPhone import

Apple Photos browsing uses Apple’s public Photos interface after you grant access to the System Photo Library. It is read-only and separate from folder indexing. USB imports are performed by Apple’s Image Capture into a folder you choose. MyMedia does not delete phone assets. A local backup copies files to your chosen destination and checks their hashes; MyMedia does not encrypt those local copies.

Experimental encrypted cloud backup

If you opt in, Google handles sign-in to our backup service at backup.uubright.com. This sign-in does not grant Google Drive access. The service maintains your account and enrolled device sessions. Session credentials are encrypted locally using operating-system-backed secure storage.

Selected file contents, filenames, original paths, timestamps and file hashes are encrypted on your Mac before upload to our service and Cloudflare R2 storage. The service can see account and device identity, opaque object keys, ciphertext hashes and sizes, counts and timing. Your recovery phrase and unwrapped master key are not sent to the service. Google sign-in cannot recover a lost phrase. Backup completion checks the encrypted index; a separate Verify action downloads and checks file contents. Restore writes verified files into a new destination.

This backup format is experimental and further security review is pending. Keep another copy of important files. Disconnecting or signing out does not delete stored backup data. Contact support for account or stored-data deletion requests; retention and deletion controls are not yet available in the desktop app. We cannot decrypt your backup without its recovery phrase; do not send that phrase to support.

Google Drive access

If you connect Google Drive, Google handles sign-in and asks you to grant the drive.readonly permission. This permits the app to see and download all files in your Drive. Folder selection limits what MyMedia processes, not the account-wide scope of Google's permission.

The integration requests file and folder metadata to display your Drive, including names, identifiers, file types, sizes, modification times, and available checksums or revisions. It fetches small preview thumbnails from Google’s thumbnail hosts and caches them locally; you can clear that cache in the app. It downloads original files only after you explicitly choose them and a local destination. The app may display account information returned by Google. It does not modify, delete, or upload files in Drive.

Drive API requests go directly between your Mac and Google. uuBright does not receive your Google credentials, Drive file contents, or Drive listing through an application server. If you add the download folder as a local photo source, photos in that folder are indexed and analyzed as described above.

Access tokens are used by the app's main process. Persistent refresh credentials are encrypted locally using Electron's operating-system-backed secure storage. They are not exposed to the photo page in the renderer.

MyMedia's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used to provide the user-facing photo features described here, not for advertising, sale, or training generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Automatic update checks

Unless you switch off update checks in About, the app automatically checks for a newer release at most once per day while it is running. It requests appcast.json from api.scimigo.com, uuBright’s service, with a static fallback at uubright.com/mymedia/appcast.json. You can also request a check manually.

The request contains no app-generated identifier, photo contents, library metadata, account details, or machine information. Like ordinary web requests, it exposes connection information such as your IP address to the receiving host and its delivery providers. Aggregate request counts may be used as a rough usage signal; they are not a count of distinct people. This check runs automatically by default, unlike the optional features below. It does not download or install an update without your action.

Apple Messages attachments

If you choose to browse Messages attachments, macOS requires Full Disk Access for MyMedia Photos. This operating-system permission is broader than the files this feature uses. The feature reads supported photo files from your local Messages attachments folder, shows previews and can compare their hashes with local originals. It does not read the Messages conversation database or message text, upload attachments, or change files in Messages. Copying chosen attachments to another folder requires your action; those copies may then be indexed locally. You can revoke Full Disk Access in macOS Privacy & Security.

Optional services and sharing

These features send data outside your Mac only when you choose to use them:

  • Place-name lookup: coordinates are sent to Apple's geocoding service to resolve place names.
  • Cloud portrait generation: a selected portrait and your script are sent to D-ID using your own API key. That provider processes the request under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • S3 folder copies: files from the folder you select are uploaded to your configured AWS S3 destination using your AWS CLI credentials. This feature does not provide MyMedia client-side encryption or restore.
  • External AI agents: if you enable local MCP access and give an agent the bearer token, it can read information exposed by the app's tools and propose cleanup plans. What that agent sends to its provider depends on your agent configuration. Applying a proposed cleanup requires an in-app human action.

Photos downloaded from Drive become local files. Choosing an optional sharing or backup feature for those files can send them to the selected service. The Drive connection itself does not initiate those uploads. Review the destination and its data policy before using an external service.

Optional model downloads

When you click a model download button, the app requests a small URL manifest from uubright.com, then downloads the chosen model from GitHub Releases or its upstream Hugging Face host. Those providers receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address. These requests do not send your photos, face embeddings, Google credentials, or Drive listing. Downloads require no model-host account, are checked against a SHA-256 checksum pinned in the app, and are stored locally on your Mac. Source attribution, licenses, and checksums accompany our model mirror release.

Retention, disconnect, and deletion

Originals and downloaded copies remain in your selected folders until you remove them. Local indexes, cached previews, face data, settings, and operation history persist in the app's local data directory until cleared through available app controls or removed from your Mac. Removing a library source is not a promise to erase every cached or indexed record.

Disconnecting Google Drive cancels active Drive operations and removes locally stored sign-in credentials. It does not delete downloaded copies or their local analysis. To revoke the Google-side grant, remove MyMedia Photos from your Google Account connections. Google retains its own service records according to its policies.

uuBright cannot remotely erase files stored only on your Mac. If you explicitly use encrypted cloud backup, we hold encrypted backup objects as described above. Contact us for help locating and clearing app data on your Mac. If you use an external generation, backup, or agent service, manage retention and deletion with that provider as well.

Website and support

The primary product website at photos.uubright.com uses Cloudflare Pages and Cloudflare delivery and security services. The uuBright company site and legacy product URL use GitHub Pages. Cloudflare may process connection information and request logs under its privacy policy. Cloudflare also adds a functional email-address decoding script to protect displayed addresses from automated collection. Google Analytics 4 is optional on these product pages and loads only after you accept website analytics. If you decline, the Google tag is not loaded. We have not added advertising pixels, session recording, signup forms, or third-party font requests. GitHub may process connection information such as IP addresses and request logs to operate the hosting service under its privacy statement.

If you email support, we receive your email address and the message or attachments you send. We use them to respond and investigate the issue. Avoid sending personal photos, tokens, or credentials unless they are necessary and you choose to share them. Support correspondence may be retained for follow-up; you can request deletion.

Security and policy changes

Credentials are protected with local secure storage, and Google requests use HTTPS. Access to your Mac, backups, or an agent you authorize can affect the privacy of local data. No storage or transmission system can guarantee absolute security.

We will update this policy when the app's data handling changes and revise the effective date. Material changes to permissions or external processing will be explained before you use the affected feature.

Public beta disk-image downloads are served from Cloudflare R2. Download requests expose ordinary connection information to Cloudflare; they do not send your photo library or desktop-app activity.

Optional website analytics

With your permission, Google Analytics measures page visits, scrolling, outgoing links, screenshot views, beta email-link clicks and future download-link clicks. It uses analytics cookies to distinguish browsers and receives information such as page URLs, campaign tags, browser and device information, and connection information. Google processes this data under its privacy policy. Google Signals, user-provided data collection and advertising personalization are disabled for this property. We do not send app activity, library searches, filenames, photos, people labels or backup details to website analytics.

Your analytics choice is stored in this browser for 90 days. Use “Analytics preferences” in the footer to change it. Declining after accepting reloads the page without the tag and removes this site’s Google Analytics cookies; it does not erase data already received by Google. Email-link clicks indicate interest, not a completed beta request. Analytics blockers and declined consent can make counts incomplete.
